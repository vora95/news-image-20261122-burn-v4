# THE LATE EDITION — Sunday 22 November 2026

One page, one day. **36 stories, 7 regions, every one dated Sunday 22 November 2026** — read from the wires up to 11:40 UTC, after the morning editions had already gone to press.

## What this page is

A broadsheet front page built the way a late edition is built: a timestamped **wire log** runs down the left side (01:00–11:40 UTC, as the news actually arrived), and the **three stories that matter** sit beside it on the right — COP31's deal that would not land, the overnight raid of 186 drones over Ukraine, and Iran's radar claim against Bahrain's denial. Below them: the ten stories that moved the world, a pin map, 23 more stories, and a date check that names everything deliberately left out.

## Why it is different

- **It is the late edition.** The morning's top entries were drawn from wires up to 05:30–08:41 UTC. This page reads wires up to **11:40 UTC** — the COP31 plenary slipping to 11:00, Hong Kong turnout near 90% by 10:26, the 08:00 Kyiv air-force count.
- **The count is right.** The wires first said 400 drones; the real overnight figure is **186 launched, 161 downed**. The page says so on its face and in its date check — "186 drones, not 400".
- **Nothing stale, nothing claimed as fact that is not.** The 400-drone barrage, the Bayelsa capsizing, El Chapo Isidro, the EU–Indonesia deal and the Kryvyi Rih strike are Friday and Saturday news. They appear only in the date check, with their real dates. Every lead and medium carries a certainty chip — REPORTED, CLAIMED, ONGOING or CONFIRMED — and names its outlet.
- **The day boundary is explained, not hidden.** A box at the foot of the page lists 13 dated exclusions, what was counted as Sunday and why, and what was still undecided at 11:40 UTC (COP31 adoption, the ATP final, Liverpool–Man Utd, the Hong Kong results).

## The three that matter

3. **Iran claims two radars hit; Bahrain denies it** — the IRGC says Patriot radars at Al-Riffa and Awali Sakhir were hit; Bahrain confirms only a civilian-site fire. CLAIM.
